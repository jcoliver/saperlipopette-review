The majority of my comments have been addressed. In regards to the vignette I 
mentioned, I had in mind something that a user (teacher or self-directed 
learner) could guide their first use of the package. The article 
"saperlipopette, a risk-free playground for learning more Git" provides 
high-level information and justification, but is not something a new user to 
saperlipopette could really use to get started. I think the introductory 
vignettes from [dplyr](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/dplyr/vignettes/dplyr.html) 
and [dbplyr](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/dbplyr/vignettes/dbplyr.html) 
do a better job showing what I am trying to explain. I think expanding the 
"Get started" vignette, borrowing from the material on Zenodo could go a long 
way to communicating worked examples of how to use the package.
